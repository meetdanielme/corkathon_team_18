How we set these prices

Priced below the tools restaurants already pay for

Forecasting and scheduling software for hospitality usually costs €40–€200 per location each month and often needs till integrations. Footfall needs no hardware or setup, so we priced it for owners, not analysts.

Does it pay for itself? Illustrative example: if one better-timed order a month avoids wasting a tray of 24 pastries at €3.50 each, that's €84 saved, more than four times the Essentials price.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need a till or POS integration?

No. Footfall works from your location, opening hours and supplier cutoffs. You can be set up in a few minutes.

What counts as a location?

Any address you trade from with its own opening hours: a shop, a second café, or a regular pitch for a food truck.

Is Footfall a sales forecast?

No. It tells you which nearby events and weather are likely to make a day busier or quieter, shows the evidence and how sure it is, and leaves the quantities to you.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly plans have no contract, and you can drop back to the free Starter plan whenever you like.
