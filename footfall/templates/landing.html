For independent cafés in Cork

Know which days will be busy before your order is due.

Footfall watches local events and the weather around your café, explains which ones matter for your opening hours, and shows you which supplier order to adjust before the cutoff.

The problem

Your busiest days are decided by what happens down the street.

  • Events you never hear about

    Graduations, matches, gigs and conferences fill the streets nearby. Nobody tells the café on the corner.

  • Weather that changes the day

    A wet afternoon empties the street and a bright one fills it, often after you've placed your order.

  • Cutoffs that don't wait

    Bakery and dairy orders close days ahead. By the time the crowd arrives, it's too late to order more.

How it works

From a noisy city to one clear decision

  1. Refresh your local picture

    In one tap, Footfall checks a curated set of Cork venue pages and the weather forecast for each of your locations.

  2. See your week, ranked

    Events within your walking radius and opening hours rise to the top. Anything that doesn't fit is shown as filtered out, with the reason.

  3. Check the evidence

    Each insight shows the source quote, when it was checked, how far away it is, the likely effect and how confident Footfall is.

  4. Act before the cutoff

    Footfall links each insight to the supplier deadline it affects and drafts a message. You edit it, approve it and copy it. Nothing is sent for you.

Honest by design

A second pair of eyes, not a crystal ball

Footfall shows its working, so you can decide how much to trust it.

  • Evidence on every card

    Every insight quotes its source page, with a link and the time it was checked.

  • Impact is a guide, not a forecast

    Low, medium and high labels come from transparent rules. Footfall never invents attendance or sales figures.

  • You stay in control

    You choose the quantities. Footfall never places orders, sends messages or spends money for you.

  • Nothing quietly dropped

    Filtered-out events, out-of-date sources and failed refreshes are shown, not hidden.
